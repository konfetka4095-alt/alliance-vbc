OUR VOLLEYBALL PROGRAMS

From beginner fundamentals to elite competitive training, Alliance Volleyball Club provides structured pathways for players of all ages and stages.

Ages 8 - 14

Youth Skill Development Clinics

Focus on fundamental volleyball mechanics: passing (forearm platform), setting, serving, attacking footwork, and basic court positioning in a fun, positive learning atmosphere.

Low coach-to-player ratio (1:6) for individualized feedback
High-repetition technical drills + mini-scrimmages
Official Alliance training t-shirt included
ScheduleSaturdays 9:30 AM - 11:30 AM
Location121 Worth Blvd, Thornhill
Duration10-Week Session
Fee$345 + HST
Ages 11 - 15

Prep for Rep (Bridge Program)

Designed specifically for athletes aspiring to make competitive Rep teams (12U-15U). Emphasizes high-speed game tempo, offensive rotations (5-1 / 6-2), jump serving, and defensive transition systems.

Pre-tryout tactical and mental preparation
Positional specialty sessions (Setters, Hitters, Libero)
Evaluation report cards with targeted growth metrics
ScheduleTuesdays & Thursdays 6:00 PM
Location121 Worth Blvd, Thornhill
Duration8-Week Cohort
Fee$420 + HST
Grades 5 - 10

Recreational House League & Games

A balanced 50/50 format: 45 minutes of skill training followed by 45 minutes of referee-officiated gameplay each week. Great for school team players and athletes looking for fun, structured match play.

Equal playing time guaranteed for all participants

Club jersey & volleyball included

Location121 Worth Blvd, Thornhill

QUESTIONS? WE'VE GOT ANSWERS.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find quick answers about Rep tryouts, youth clinics, season fees, uniform packages, practice locations, and club policies.

Tryouts take place each September for the upcoming Ontario Volleyball Association (OVA) season. Athletes participate in skill evaluation drills and match play across 2 evaluation sessions. In accordance with OVA tryout regulations, offers of commitment are extended following the completion of tryout windows.

Rep teams train 2 to 3 times per week (2 hours each session) plus weekly strength & agility conditioning. Teams participate in 4 to 5 regular season OVA tournaments (held on Saturdays or Sundays) leading up to the 3-day Ontario Provincial Championships in April/May.

We recommend our Youth Skill Development Clinics or Recreational House League! These programs focus on teaching fundamental mechanics (passing, setting, serving) in an encouraging, low-pressure setting where athletes build confidence.

Youth Clinics focus primarily on beginner to intermediate fundamentals. Prep for Rep is an accelerated bridge program for athletes who already have basic volleyball experience and want to learn competitive offensive systems (5-1 rotations), high-speed defense, and jump serving to make a Rep team roster.

Rep club fees cover gym rental time, head coach & assistant coach honorariums, OVA tournament entry fees, Provincial Championship registration, speed/agility coaching, official athlete uniform package (jerseys, warm-up shirts, backpack), and club insurance.

Our primary facility is located at 121 Worth Blvd, Thornhill, ON L4J 8K1. The facility features sprung maple hardwood floors, high ceilings, and ample free parking for parents and athletes.
