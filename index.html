BUILT FOR PLAYER DEVELOPMENT

Alliance Volleyball Club provides structured youth training and competitive Rep Team opportunities for athletes across York Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

OUR PURPOSE

A CLUB BUILT AROUND THE ATHLETE

Alliance was created to give young athletes a clear place to learn, improve and compete. Our training combines strong technical foundations with game understanding, confidence and positive team habits.

Whether an athlete is beginning their volleyball journey or preparing for competitive Rep play, our goal is to provide purposeful instruction, constructive feedback and an environment that supports long-term development.

Athlete-Centred

Every decision begins with the athlete’s development, well-being and overall experience.

Development With Direction

Every session has clear objectives and a meaningful progression from fundamentals to competition.

WHAT UNITES US

OPEN POSITION 2026–27 SEASON

VOLLEYBALL COACH

Lead practices, support athlete development and help create a focused, positive and competitive team culture.

  • Work collaboratively with other Alliance coaches and club staff
  • Represent Alliance with professionalism, respect and consistency

ASSISTANT COACH

Support the lead coach during practices and competition while helping athletes stay engaged, organized and confident.
